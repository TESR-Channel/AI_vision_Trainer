1 · Choose Your Task

What should the AI do? You can switch anytime — your photos are kept.

🎯 Object Detection — what & where

You draw a box on each photo; the AI learns to find the object, draw its box and report its center position (x, y) live — ready for robots and automation. Tracks one object per frame. Recommended: 40+ labeled photos per class (80+ is noticeably better).

🏷 Classification — what is it

The fastest path: no boxes to draw. The AI names what it sees in the whole frame. Great for sorting, go/no-go checks and quick demos. Recommended: 30+ photos per class (60+ is noticeably better).

2 · Classes

What should the AI learn to tell apart? At least 2 classes — and adding a "background" class of your empty scene makes the "none" answer much more reliable.

Detect mode shows the box, a crosshair at the object's center, and its position in pixels and percent — the numbers a robot arm or conveyor system needs. One object per frame; for multi-object production systems, use the TESR Desktop Trainer (YOLO).
